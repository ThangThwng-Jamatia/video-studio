# How to Build a Well (pilot, 2026-10-25)

Voice: Brian, atempo 1.14 + silence trim → 62.5 s. Job `build-well`, code `_engine/src/build/BuildWell.tsx`.

## Script
If you dig a hole in the ground until you hit water, you have a well, but the walls keep collapsing and bury the water in mud. So you line the hole with a ring of stacked stones. This is a casing. But now rain washes dirt and animal waste straight down into your water and everyone gets sick. So you build a stone wall around the top, waist high. This is a wellhead. But the bucket keeps falling in and your rope snaps on the sharp stone edge. So you put two posts over the hole and a log across them with a crank. This is a windlass. But in summer the water drops below your bucket. So you dig deeper into the layer of rock that stays wet all year. This is an aquifer. But the water at the bottom is cloudy with sand. So you pour gravel at the bottom to hold the sand down. This is a gravel pack. But leaves and birds still fall in. So you build a little roof over the top. And that roof is not decoration. It blocks the sunlight so algae can't grow in your water. Add a bucket, a frog who lives on the ledge, and someone who throws coins in for luck. Congratulations, you have built a well.

## Fix chain
hole → casing → wellhead → windlass → aquifer (dig deeper) → gravel pack → roof. Reveal: the roof is not decoration, it blocks sunlight so algae can't grow.
