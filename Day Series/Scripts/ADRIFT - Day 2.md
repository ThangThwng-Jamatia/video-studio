## Fact sources and editorial notes
- [Maritime NZ — Distress flares](https://www.maritimenz.govt.nz/recreational-craft/getting-started/before-you-go/communications/distress-flares/): parachute flares burn 40–60 seconds; fire one at a time; read device instructions and fire downwind, clear of the raft. Signal animation is time-compressed, not a real-time burn or a firing tutorial.
- [AMSEA — Life Raft Survival](https://www.amsea.org/life-raft-survival): explicitly corrects the old no-water-for-24-hours instruction. Stay hydrated from the start. Canopy and ventilation help manage heat; avoid unnecessary exertion; collect rain with the raft system; secure supplies against loss in waves; bail water from the raft.
- Day 1's no-drinking instruction is corrected explicitly in this episode. Do not repeat its claim that withholding water activates a protective water-saving mode.
- Ship failing to notice, weather, consumption, and wave are fictional story events. No fixed flare visibility distance or guaranteed rescue is asserted.
- Cliffhanger choices: A KEEP COLLECTING / B BAIL NOW. Day 3 should resolve to bailing while preserving the collection system if possible.
