## Facts + sources
- Cash-in-transit boxes use Intelligent Banknote Neutralisation Systems (IBNS): forced opening triggers dye/ink that permanently stains the notes (European Central Bank, Decision ECB/2013/10 on IBNS; Europol). Stained notes are flagged by banks.
- Form 8300: a trade or business receiving more than $10,000 in cash in one or related transactions must file with the IRS within 15 days, with the payer's name, address and TIN (IRS, 26 U.S.C. §6050I).
- Casinos must file a Currency Transaction Report for cash transactions over $10,000 in a gaming day (31 CFR §1021.311). Cage surveillance is standard (state gaming regs; the "40 cameras" is a dramatised count, kept as story, not a stat).
