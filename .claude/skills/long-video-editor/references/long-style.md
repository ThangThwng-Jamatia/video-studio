## 1. The non-negotiables (all 3 agree)
1. **No running subtitles.** Text on screen = keywords, stacked lists, titles, labels. (One exception in IFO: a single quoted line as a small subtitle with a red underline.)
2. **The hook is a motion-graphics showcase.** In 0–50 s a full-screen graphic or overlay is on screen **50–80 % of the time**; after the hook, density drops to ~1 graphic beat every 15–30 s plus chapter cards.
3. **Every hook sentence gets its own visual idea.** Never two sentences in a row on plain A-roll in the first 50 s.
4. **Frame 0 is never plain.** Text or graphic by 0.5 s (IFO title, CVC tags), or a visual twist by 3 s (IMAN real→cartoon).
5. **Continuous music bed** under the whole video (voice-gap floor −29 … −38 dBFS → bed ≈ 15–20 dB under the voice). SFX on nearly every graphic entrance.
6. **Warm, cinematic A-roll grade**, shallow depth of field, practical lights in the background (lamps, sconces). Graphics carry the brand colour, not the A-roll.
7. **Graphics explain, they don't decorate.** Each one shows the idea of the sentence (node graph = "automate my editing", money down a drain = "losing money", ticking clock = "hours editing").

## 2. Pacing (measured, scene-detect threshold 0.22; overlay changes come on top)

|---|---|---|
| IMAN | **1.7 s** | 3.5 s |
| CVC | 2.9 s | 7.5 s (screen recordings later) |

**Targets:** hook ≤ 2.5 s per visual change (counting overlays), body 3–6 s, never > 10 s without a framing change, B-roll, or text beat.

## 5. Typography (3 families max per video; all already in `@remotion/google-fonts`)
| Role | Font | Spec (at 1080p) |
|---|---|---|
| Keyword stacks, titles, chapter cards (Look A) | **Sora** 600–700 | 64–90 px, tracking −0.02 em, white, soft shadow; the line being spoken is 100 %, older lines 100 % (no dimming) |
| UI text, pills, cards, Look B/C titles | **Inter Tight** 500–800 | pills 34–40 px 600; card titles 40–48 px 700; hero words 140–220 px 800 with glow `0 0 30px rgba(255,255,255,.35)` |
| Script accent (1 word per stack, the emotional word) | **Mr Dafoe** | 1.3–1.5 × the stack size; Look A in neon blue with 2-layer glow; Look B in white with chromatic aberration (R/B offset 2–3 px) |
Emphasis inside a stack = Inter Tight/Sora **italic** (IFO "*is going to*", "*something*", CVC "*hours*", "*filming*" in accent colour).

## 6. Motion vocabulary
**Text**
- **Word-append with blur-in** (default): each word appears on its spoken frame, 6 frames, blur 10 → 0 px, y +12 → 0, opacity 0 → 1. Lines stack downward, left-aligned, top-left (x ≈ 0.04–0.06 W, y from 0.07 H) or beside the head.
- **Typewriter** for pill tags and UI text: 1 char / frame, cursor bar.
- **Script write-on**: left-to-right mask wipe 0.5–0.8 s, slight overshoot.
- **Counters** roll with ease-out over 0.8–1.5 s (money, subs, %, clock); the last digits blur while rolling.
- **Head-between title**: two huge words placed either side of the face box (use the facebox helper), optional second line under the chin.
- Exit: blur-out 6 frames, or the whole graphic leaves with the shot cut.

**Cards / UI**
- Spring pop-in: scale 0.85 → 1, opacity, blur 8 → 0, `spring({damping: 14, mass: 0.6})`, stagger 3–5 frames.
- Node graph: centre node first, connectors draw (stroke-dashoffset) 8–12 frames each, end cards pop as their line arrives.
- Phone mockups: rounded 48 px, inner UI animates (ring, timer, typing). Up to 3 side by side, in sync.
- Recurring tracker (IMAN tier list S–F: S `#f87171`, A `#fb923c`, B `#facc15`, C `#4ade80`, D `#60a5fa`, E `#a78bfa`, F `#e879f9`): comes back after every item with the new icon dropping into its row. Any list/ranking video gets one (tier list, checklist, progress lanes).
- Persistent chapter label: neon script, bottom-left (x 0.02 W, y 0.93 H, ≈ 40 px tall), on for the whole chapter.
- Chapter card: Look bg, "Side-Hustle #N" Sora 600 centred at y 0.15, horizontal carousel of 5 glossy icon tiles (centre tile lit), neon script title write-on under it. 3–4 s, whoosh + swell_hit.

**Transitions** (hard cut is the default, ~80 %)
| Transition | Where | Spec |
|---|---|---|
| Angle switch (the "smooth cut") | every sentence boundary | IMAN alternates wide ↔ close camera. With one camera: alternate full frame ↔ 1.25–1.35× crop on the face, cut only between sentences; jump cuts inside a sentence get hidden by the next crop change |
| Card shrink / grow | into and out of full-screen graphics | A-roll scales to 0.55 into a 24 px rounded card with 1 px border + shadow on the Look bg, 12–15 frames `Easing.inOut(cubic)`; reverse to come back |
| White flash | impact moments (hammer hit, reveal) | 2–3 frames of white, impact SFX |
| Stylised dissolve | real → 3D/stylised version of the same moment | 10–12 frame crossfade |
| Push-in to transition | inside 3D | camera dollies into an object (slot reel, screen), then cut on the frame where it fills the view |
| Z-depth fly-through | collages | cards spread on z, camera moves through, 1–1.5 s |
| Whip / motion blur | energy changes (rare) | 4–6 frames directional blur |
| B&W cut | emphasis line | desaturate the alternate angle for one sentence |

## 7. IMAN-style 3D (the "explanation" scenes: 0:58, 1:27–2:00 and the hook)
> **User rule 2026-10-19:** three.js primitives of organic things (brains, people, globes, box batteries, slab phones) read as cartoonish. Build only clean product objects (glass, gold, metal, devices) with `PremiumGfx` (env map + physical materials), and do the rest as 2.5D depth panels (components.md → v2) or real B-roll. The table below is the *metaphor list*, not a build list.
What they are: stylised, warmly lit 3D **object metaphors** acting out the sentence, 2.5–5 s each, one slow camera move each, often with a neon script word or a Sora title. In the hook ~50 % of the time; in the body ~1 per chapter for the first 3 minutes, then mostly the tier list.
| Spoken idea | Scene | Key motion |
|---|---|---|
| "the one / the opportunity / the answer" | dark stage, podium, red velvet drape with gold emblem | spotlight fades up 0 → 1 over 1 s, push-in, gold bold title pops |
| "build / foundation / brick by brick" | gold bricks stamped with letters | bricks fly in on arcs and stack into a pyramid spelling the word |
| "money / the internet makes money" | wooden table under a top light | bill stacks rain down, bounce, pile up |
| "you're losing / burning money" | drain grate in swirling water | bills spiral inward and sink |
| "random / gamble / ranking N items" | slot machine on a dark blurred casino | reels spin, camera pushes into the window, window becomes a meter/tier bar |
| "turn raw into value / AI is a tool" | anvil labelled with the tool | hammer strike → white flash + spark burst → glowing ingot tumbling on navy |
| "it looks valuable but isn't" | a bill / price tag | colour turns red, number counts to 0 |
| "good vs bad" | the subject dimmed in the centre | ✓ left label, dashed line, ✗ right label |
| "bluffing / branding / hype" | poker table, cards, chips | cards slide to the centre, neon word over them |
**Look recipe (three.js):** background `#07080b` fog 8 → 28; ambient 0.25; one warm key spotlight `#ffcf8a` with a visible cone (additive cone mesh, opacity 0.12) and soft shadows; rim light cool `#6b8cff` 0.4; materials: velvet `#7a1020` roughness 0.9, gold `#d4a64a` metalness 1 roughness 0.28 + emissive for "glowing", paper money `#a9c99a` with a canvas-texture print, wood `#6b4a2e`; fake depth of field = blurred background plane + soft vignette; camera `Easing.inOut(sin)` push-in 6–12 %. Text over 3D: Sora (titles), Mr Dafoe neon (labels).
**The avatar:** IMAN's Pixar-style avatar is AI image-to-video footage, not something three.js can make. Use it only if the user supplies avatar clips (`B-Rolls/Avatar/`). Otherwise stage the scene without a person, or with the stylised figure from behind (over-the-shoulder, like the podium shot).

## 8. Audio
- **Music bed** for the whole video, ducked under the voice to about −20 dB relative; it rises slightly on chapter cards and at the end. `Music/` is empty: ask the user for a track once, else generate (`scripts/gen_music_cine.py` style; a long bed needs a calm 80–100 bpm pulse with sections every 30–60 s).
- **SFX map** (library `SFX/`): card/screen fly-in `whoosh` · chapter card `whoosh_long` + `swell_hit` · pill tag / icon pop `pop` or `click` · typed text `click` at 30–40 % volume per word (not per letter) · checkmark / list tick `ding` · counter roll `riser` (short) · big reveal / hammer / flash `impact` or `boom` · glitch title `glitch`. Volume 0.2–0.45; never two SFX within 0.15 s.
- Master −14 LUFS (`pipeline.py master`); the inspos sit at −14.6 / −15.6 LUFS.
