# Zombie Economy (Short, 9:16, ~1:12)
Topic: How a pandemic like a zombie virus affects the economy.
Voice: George. ~167 words.

## Hook
If a zombie virus hit tomorrow, the zombies wouldn't be the scariest part. The economy would be.

## Number one: supply breaks
Number one. Supply breaks. Truck drivers stay home. Shelves go empty. Fewer goods, more people panic-buying. So a can of beans that cost two dollars now costs twenty.

## Number two: jobs vanish
Number two. Jobs vanish. Malls, cinemas and restaurants close. People stop earning, so they stop spending, and every shop that relied on them closes too. One empty wallet empties the next one.

## Number three: markets crash
Number three. Panic hits the stock market. Investors sell everything at once, and prices crash in days. Your parents' retirement savings? Cut in half.

## Number four: printing money
Number four. The government prints money to help. That keeps people fed, but too much new cash makes every dollar worth less. That's inflation.

## Winners
Meanwhile, a few businesses boom: canned food, medicine, delivery apps... and baseball bats.

## Reality check
This isn't just fiction. In twenty twenty, real economies did the same thing, just without the zombies.

## Takeaway
The lesson: always keep an emergency fund, because when the world shuts down, cash buys you time.
